<b>Offline Currency Converter</b> is a free and open-source currency converter for Android. Convert between 160+ currencies using the latest downloaded exchange rates — even when you are offline.

Ideal for travel, shopping, international payments, or anyone who needs a simple currency calculator without relying on a permanent internet connection.

<b>Offline currency conversion</b><br>
Download exchange rates and perform currency conversions directly on your device. Once rates are synchronized, conversions work completely offline.

<b>Exchange rates</b><br>
Rates are provided by the Frankfurter API using European Central Bank reference data. Choose how often rates are synchronized, from every 6 hours to weekly, or update them manually.

<b>Features</b><br>
<ul>
<li><b>Currency converter</b> — convert between 160+ currencies</li>
<li><b>Offline mode</b> — convert currencies without an internet connection after syncing</li>
<li><b>Exchange rates</b> — use European Central Bank reference rates</li>
<li><b>Favorites</b> — quickly access your most-used currencies</li>
<li><b>Multi-currency conversion</b> — compare several currencies at once</li>
<li><b>Historical exchange rates</b> — view 7, 30, and 90-day rate charts</li>
<li><b>Exchange rate charts</b> — interactive charts with date and rate information</li>
<li><b>Home screen widget</b> — see a currency pair directly from your home screen</li>
<li><b>Automatic synchronization</b> — keep exchange rates up to date in the background</li>
<li><b>Manual synchronization</b> — update rates whenever you want</li>
<li><b>Privacy-focused</b> — no account, no ads, no tracking, and no analytics</li>
<li><b>Open source</b> — licensed under AGPLv3</li>
<li><b>13 languages</b> — ar, de, es, fr, hi, it, ja, ko, nl, pl, pt, ru, zh</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b><br>
Currency conversion happens on your device. Network access is only used to synchronize exchange rates. No account is required and the app does not contain advertising, analytics, or tracking.

<b>Free and open source</b><br>
Offline Currency Converter is free software released under the AGPLv3 license.
